# Ender Starter Fighting Staff — Production Brief

Status: **VISUAL / ANIMATION CANDIDATE — MAIN WEAPON LOCKED, EXACT ITEM DETAILS PROVISIONAL**

## Locked authority

- Ender's starting/main weapon family is **fighting staff**.
- The staff is a physical combat weapon, not a required magical focus.
- Rift Scholar magic remains usable independently of the staff unless a future specific ability explicitly requires a weapon.
- Ender's secondary/ranged weapon remains OPEN.
- Production art must preserve Ender's creator-approved likeness: very short dark copper-brown/auburn hair, bright green eyes, light freckles, brass/bronze forehead goggles with green lenses, olive/green + tan/cream field-scholar clothing language.

## Starter staff visual target

The first staff should look **simple, durable, practical, and upgradeable** rather than legendary.

Candidate construction:
- straight hardwood/rootwood core;
- roughly Ender's height or slightly taller;
- modest brass/bronze ferrules on both ends;
- wrapped central grip with two-hand spacing;
- one restrained aether-tech fitting near the upper third for later upgrade compatibility;
- no crystal orb;
- no oversized wizard headpiece;
- no glowing permanent blade;
- no ceremonial crown or religious iconography.

The silhouette must read as a fighting staff first. Any aether-tech detail is secondary.

## Combat language

Ender should visibly use genuine staff mechanics:

1. **Ready / guard** — offset two-hand grip, staff diagonal across the body.
2. **Jab / thrust** — fast linear reach using the rear hand to drive.
3. **Horizontal strike** — compact hip-driven sweep.
4. **Reverse strike** — opposite end follows through rather than resetting to a one-ended club.
5. **Low sweep** — staff travels close to the ground without turning into a magic arc.
6. **High guard / deflection** — defensive roof-line block.
7. **Vault / reposition candidate** — staff may support movement later, but this is not yet a locked combat command.
8. **Recovery** — return to a compact scholar-traveler stance rather than a martial-arts exhibition pose.

## Animation implications for the 64×96 pack

### Idle
Staff rests in a compact diagonal or vertical carry. Keep the tip within the cell with at least 1px clearance.

### Walk
Staff travels with the body and shows restrained secondary motion. Avoid a large pendulum swing that causes cell-edge clipping.

### Talk
Staff can remain grounded or tucked close. Ender's gesturing should still come from his free hand/head posture.

### Focus
Focus animation must **not** imply that the staff is required to cast. A valid focus pose may:
- hold the staff neutrally while Ender performs the Rift action himself; or
- briefly use the staff as a stabilizing/measurement tool.
The animation must still read as "Ender using Rift Scholar ability" rather than "magic emitted by wizard staff."

### Attack — 8-frame baseline
1. compact ready stance;
2. load / weight shift;
3. first-end thrust or short strike;
4. committed contact frame;
5. reverse-end follow-through;
6. recovery sweep;
7. regain grounded stance;
8. return toward idle.

The first production attack should be **physical staff combat**, not an aether slash.

## Upgrade compatibility

The starter weapon should visually support later workshop evolution without requiring a total silhouette replacement. Candidate upgradeable regions:
- end ferrules;
- grip;
- central reinforcement;
- small aether-tech fitting;
- optional later inlays/modules.

This supports the broader design direction where party-developed weapons can evolve over time and rare components may be integrated into stronger forms.

- Ender's hair remains very short dark copper-brown/auburn;
