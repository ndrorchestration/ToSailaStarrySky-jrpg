## First continuous native review chain

This draft extends [verified entry-to-ocean and empty reserve #545](https://github.com/ndrorchestration/ToSailtheStarrySky-jrpg/pull/545). The player begins at the actual project main scene (legacy untouched), presses F8 for the Ionia-first revised route, reads Ionia's hiring flyer and accepts provisional paid work, boards, completes three engine inspections and safety checks, presses F for the separate RigidBody2D test flight, and chooses a controlled water descent after phase/fuel alarms. Godot 2D physics must detect `Area2D.body_entered` water contact and settled flotation before E enables the reserve diagnosis.

**New, explicitly separated safety sequence:** The player presses E **after** the fuel reserve is inspected to request safe recovery; a second E explicitly stages a **safe berth review**. This is a **narrative/UX fixture only**: no tow, sailing route, rescue boat, travel time or geolocated harbor is simulated or established. The review must show a location-unassigned caption so players/testers do not confuse it with approved world geography. This delay and explicit safety confirmation prevent Lyra and Talen being placed aboard the test flight or magically appearing while the Wake is still adrift.

**Walkable postflight meeting:** At the review berth, Ender approaches Lyra and hears a mandatory conversation involving Ionia and Lyra about the splashdown, the crew's safety, Talen's concern and Ender's new engineer role. **Lyra is not added to the party until the full family dialogue finishes**. The safe Talen area offers an optional, gentle interaction around his block-built ship and its extra engines. Talen does not join battle or the active story party, and no special abilities or supernatural clues are attributed to him. Lyra volunteers to help solve the mechanical problem and participates as a family member, not simply as a cue-producing party object.

**Collaborative investigation:** Three distinct, physically reachable clue stations can be visited in any order, after Lyra joins: (1) phase chart/Ender's under-load drift, (2) Ionia's account of Denith's incomplete reserve log, (3) Lyra's independent perception of a note that fails to settle. Any **two of three** permit the *proposed* round-table conversation, not a confirmed fuel recipe. Ender states a stabilization hypothesis, Ionia notes Denith's incomplete information and insists on a safe supply, and Lyra participates in choosing the next investigation. The real opening model transitions to `working_theory` only at the end of that complete dialogue.

**Connected repaired-core proof:** At the theory table after this conversation, E enters the existing [physical-pickup and interactive melodic workshop #533](https://github.com/ndrorchestration/ToSailtheStarrySky-jrpg/pull/533). Unlike the standalone workshop fixture, this handoff calls `configure_from_working_theory()` with the **identical actual `Opening` instance** that began at the user's Ionia job interview and has subsequently passed water, reserve, family and clue stages. The workshop must refuse early, forged, no-Lyra, no-safe-reunion or insufficient-clue handoffs. Only then does the player retrieve two **anonymous placeholder** parts, install them, observe a failed restart, listen as Ionia nervously recalls Denith's shanty, test relative interval/note-hold hypotheses with keyboard/gamepad and visual text, receive Lyra's independent confirmation and Ionia's containment authorization, and measure a **review bench candidate**. Actual flight and combat Art execution remain **false**. The original numbers/audio remain noncanonical QA fixtures. No singing/microphone dependency.
